Q13. What is LCL?

LCL (Less than Container Load), also called consolidation or groupage, means your cargo shares a container with other shipments. It is ideal for smaller volumes that do not fill a full container. FCL means you book a full container for your goods only.

Q14. What is FCL?

FCL (Full Container Load) means you book an entire container (for example 20ft or 40ft) for your cargo. It is typically used for larger volumes or when you need exclusive use of the container.
